PhD research concept · in progress

Mechanical context and directional cues at an exposed GBR interface

Qualitative, rule-based models examining two related but biologically distinct questions in the context of early GBR membrane exposure: whether gingival fibroblast responses to microgrooves depend on ECM mechanical state, and how wound geometry and competing directional cues may influence collective epithelial re-closure. The models are hypothesis-building tools, not biologically calibrated predictions.
